Where this phase stands

A folder of 264 reference screenshots turned up after these five were built — desktop and mobile captures of direct-response landing pages, organised into exactly these categories. They are a different genre from what is above: announcement bars, numbered benefit listicles, press logos, risk-free-trial blocks.

The five designs here are brand and boutique sites. They stand on their own, but the remaining work should be built from the references rather than continuing this direction — so it is on hold pending that decision.

How every page was checked

Screenshots hide the most common phone bug — sideways scroll — so each page is measured in the browser instead, at 375, 768 and 1280 pixels wide. The audit looks for horizontal overflow, elements escaping the viewport, text clipped by a fixed height, tap targets under 44 pixels, sticky bars eating the screen, and images that failed to load.

Caught

3px of sideways scroll

A stray closing tag pushed the buy column out of its container on six product pages. Invisible in a screenshot.

Caught

Counters freezing mid-count

Animated figures could stop partway and display a wrong number. They now always settle on the true value.

Caught

Content stuck invisible

Scroll animations could leave whole sections permanently hidden. A failsafe now guarantees they appear.
