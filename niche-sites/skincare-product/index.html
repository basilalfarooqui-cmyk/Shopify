How to use it

Three steps, morning and night.

1

Cleanse and leave damp

Do not dry your face completely. Bloom spreads further and absorbs better onto skin that is still slightly wet.

2

Three or four drops

Press it in rather than rubbing. Cheeks first, then forehead, then whatever is left over the nose and chin.

3

Moisturiser, then SPF

Seal it in. In the morning finish with sunscreen — barrier repair undone by UV is barrier repair wasted.

The texture

Thin, slightly slippery, gone in a minute.

It is a watery gel rather than an oil, so it disappears instead of sitting on top. Makeup goes over it without rolling, which is the test most serums fail.

One 30 ml bottle lasts about two months at four drops, twice a day.
