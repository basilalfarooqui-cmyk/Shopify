Measure from the bony bump at the base of your neck to the top of your hip bones. Under 43 cm take Short, 43 to 51 cm Regular, above that Long. If you are between sizes, size down — a pack that rides slightly high is far more comfortable than one that sits on your backside.

Rolled four times and clipped, the main compartment will keep a sleeping bag dry through a full day of monsoon rain. It is not a dry bag — do not submerge it — and the outer pockets are not waterproof at all, deliberately, so wet things have somewhere to go.

Post it to us and we will fix it, whatever went wrong and however long ago you bought it. Twelve days on average, returned free. If we cannot repair it we will tell you honestly rather than sell you a new one.
