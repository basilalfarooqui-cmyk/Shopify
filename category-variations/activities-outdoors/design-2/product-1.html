Measure C7 (the bony bump at the base of the neck) to the iliac crest. S: under 43 cm. R: 43–51 cm. L: above 51 cm. Between sizes, take the shorter — a pack riding high is more comfortable than one sitting low.

Hydrostatic head is the water column the fabric holds before it leaks. 1,800 mm handles sustained heavy rain. It is not a dry bag rating and we do not claim it is: submerge the pack and water will get in through the roll closure.

Covered: seams, stitching, buckles, zips, fabric delamination, frame failure. Not covered: cuts, burns, crampon damage, rodent damage. Those are repaired at cost, quoted in writing before any work starts.
