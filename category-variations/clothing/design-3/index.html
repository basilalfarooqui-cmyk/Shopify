3 Reasons To Replace Your Everyday Tee

Written by the people who make it. Read it in ninety seconds.

1. It is twice the weight

A supermarket tee is 140 to 160 gsm. Ours is 240. That is the single difference that decides whether a shirt hangs properly on your shoulders or clings to everything underneath it, and whether you can see your skin through the back in daylight.

Heavier cotton also survives washing. The thin ones do not get thin over time — they were always thin, and the washing just makes it obvious.

Shop the tee

2. The neck actually holds

Almost every tee fails at the collar first. It stretches, goes wavy, and the shirt is finished even though the body is fine. Ours has a double-ribbed neck taped shoulder to shoulder with a strip of the same cotton, so the weight of the shirt hangs from the tape rather than from the collar.

We tested it at 200 wash cycles. The collar measured within two millimetres of where it started.

3. It comes back the same size

Garment dyed, then pre-shrunk twice before it is cut. Most makers shrink once, which leaves another two per cent to give up in your machine — enough to turn a medium into something that no longer covers your waistband.

Measured across ten wash cycles, ours moves under two per cent. Buy your normal size and it stays your normal size.
