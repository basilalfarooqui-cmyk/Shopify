How to make it

  1. One heaped teaspoon into a mug.
  2. A splash of just-boiled water, then froth until there are no lumps.
  3. Top with hot water, or half water and half milk if you want it richer.
  4. A pinch of salt if you drink it black. It sounds wrong and it is not.
