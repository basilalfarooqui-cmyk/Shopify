What the week is actually like

Day 1 Tastes like thin spiced chocolate. You will probably think it needs to be sweeter. Do not add sugar yet.
Day 2–3 If you dropped coffee entirely, this is the headache. Halve your coffee instead of stopping and it mostly does not happen.
Day 4 The taste stops registering as wrong. Most people who quit do so before this point, which is the reason the trial is seven days and not three.
Day 5–6 The flat spot around three in the afternoon is usually gone. This is the part people write in about.
Day 7 You will know. If it is a no, that is a perfectly good outcome and it cost you ₹490.
