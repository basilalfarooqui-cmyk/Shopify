Carbonated water, chicory root fibre, lemon juice from concentrate, cassava root fibre, cane sugar, sea salt, stevia leaf, natural lemon flavour. That is the whole list, and it is deliberately short enough to read in the shop.

Most people eat about half the fibre they should. A can of this is roughly a third of the daily gap. Start with one a day rather than three — the first week can be noisy if your gut is not used to it, which is worth saying plainly rather than in small print.

Dispatched in 24 hours, two to four days across India. Store cool and out of sun; it keeps nine months unopened. Chill for at least four hours before drinking — warm, the fibre reads as slightly syrupy.
