How it's built

Welded, not stitched, where the water gets in.

Every seam on the outer shell is radio-frequency welded rather than sewn, because a stitch is a row of holes. The zips are polyurethane coated. The base is a single moulded piece with no join at all.

It is more expensive to make and it is the reason we can put ten years behind it without worrying.
