On leather

Vegetable tanning takes six weeks. Chrome takes a day.

Almost all leather sold today is chrome-tanned, because it is fast and it comes out the same colour every time. It also never really changes — it wears out rather than wearing in.

Ours sits in oak-bark pits for six weeks. It arrives pale and stiff, darkens where your hands go, and softens along the folds. Two of the same bag will not look alike after a year, which is rather the point.

Notes

How this works

01

Made after you order

Nothing sits in a warehouse. Your piece is cut once you buy it, which takes about three weeks and means we never discount stock we could not sell.

02

Repaired, not replaced

Send anything back at any point in its life and we will restitch, re-edge or replace the hardware. There is no time limit and no charge.

03

One price, always

We do not run sales. The price in March is the price in November, which is the only honest way to sell something made to order.
