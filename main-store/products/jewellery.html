Sizing

How to choose a length

16 inch sits at the base of the throat and works under a shirt collar. 18 inch — the one most people pick — falls just below the collarbone. 20 and 22 inch drop onto the sternum and layer well over a knit. If you are between two, take the longer one; the extender takes it back up.
